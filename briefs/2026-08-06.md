## What Happened

### The bank improved its forecast and spent nothing

The Reserve Bank held the repo rate at **5.25%** on Wednesday — a fourth consecutive hold, unanimous, with the Neutral stance retained. The rate was never the variance and the statement was. **It cut its FY27 inflation projection to 5.0% from 5.1%, and it raised its FY27 growth projection to 6.7% from 6.6%**, attributing the inflation cut primarily to cheaper crude. A bank that lowers its inflation forecast and raises its growth forecast in one statement is describing a better trade-off, and it declined to spend a cut on saying so. That is optionality held for **5–7 October**. The uncomfortable number is the one nobody quotes: **inflation is forecast to peak at 5.9% in Q3 FY27**, 190 basis points above target, and it is being called food and fuel.

### Nine basis points is not reassurance, it is indifference

**Across both post-decision sessions the Nifty moved +0.09% in total** — Wednesday +0.04%, today +0.05%. Carriers wrote that the hold "broadly reassured" investors, which is generous. A market that has priced a decision does not react to it, and this one did not. What decided today's close sat below the index: **thirty of forty-one Nifty 50 constituents fell, the median one by 0.57%, and the index finished green because Reliance rose 3.52%.** No cause was located for that move — our close is materially above every intraday figure any carrier quoted mid-session, so it finished much stronger than it traded. Underneath, six stocks fell 5% or more and forty-two rose 3% or more. This was an earnings-dispersion session wearing a flat headline.

### Blue Star sold thirteen percent more and kept less

Blue Star (BLUESTARCO), a holding, filed at 14:32 into a live session and fell **5.65% to ₹1,571.00**. **Revenue grew 13.3% to ₹3,377.92 crore while operating profit fell 12.3% and the margin went 6.7% to 5.2%.** The damage is concentrated and it is in the segment that carries the story: **room air conditioners and commercial refrigeration grew 12.7% and their segment margin halved, 5.8% to 2.9%.** Profit was ₹102.51 crore against ₹120.82, and **₹9.2 crore of that is a one-off property gain, so the underlying fall is nearer 23% than 15%.** Management blames a short summer, old inventory sitting in the trade, metal costs and West Asia, and attaches a cautious outlook for the rest of the year. **The stock is 3.30% above its 52-week low.**

### Garware's best quarter ever bought it nothing

Garware Hi-Tech Films (GRWRHITECH), a holding, filed the best quarter in its history at 12:46 and closed **down 0.96% at ₹7,338.50**. **Revenue ₹633 crore, up 28%; operating margin through 30% for the first time at 30.3%; profit ₹133 crore, up 60%.** Nothing in it weakens the thesis. The price is telling you about positioning, not about the company: **the stock is up 41.47% in three months and 72.33% this year, and Wednesday's close was its 52-week high of ₹7,409.50.** The run happened inside the session we never saw; today is the give-back. One thing is unresolved and it is not in the numbers. **The auditor's conclusion on the 39-page filing is unread** — those pages are images and did not survive extraction — so nothing about the audit opinion is asserted here.

### Waaree Renewable's newest customer is its own parent

Waaree Renewable (WAAREERTL), a holding, took a **210 MWp solar construction order worth ₹210 crore on Wednesday from Solaris Horizon Energy — a step-down subsidiary of Waaree Energies, its own parent.** The company disclosed it as a related-party transaction at arm's length, in the announcement itself. **That closes a question this desk has asked for five runs, and closes it against the optimistic reading** — but it is not a scandal and should not be written as one. The disclosure standard was applied in public. **What changes is the question:** no longer whether the revenue is external, but how related-party work is priced, and **₹1.0 crore per MWp is now a hard reference number against which the group's third-party pricing can be checked.** The 1,082 MWp award of 27-July still has no named buyer.
