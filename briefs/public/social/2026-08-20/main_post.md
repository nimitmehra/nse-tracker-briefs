- The US Treasury said on Wednesday it will at least double the buybacks of its long-dated bonds, from $2 billion to a minimum of $4 billion an operation, and long-term American yields fell the same session, the 10-year to 4.647% and the 30-year by nearly a tenth of a percentage point to 5.196%.
