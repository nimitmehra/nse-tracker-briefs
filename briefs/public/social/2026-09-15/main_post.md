- Brent rose above US$107 after a drone strike shut the Saudi pipeline around the closed Strait of Hormuz, the US 10-year yield touched 5%, and the Reserve Bank auctions ₹1 trillion of bonds from Thursday to drain surplus cash, more supply though not a rate rise.

- India imports most of its oil, so the rupee fell to a seven-week low, 95.945, the 10-year yield rose to about 7.1% and foreign investors sold ₹2,977.90 crore net, a fifth straight day, while only IT rose, up 2.19%.

- Solar Industries lost 13.64% after agreeing to pay ₹12,951 crore in cash for South Africa's Omnia without saying how it will be funded.

- The Fed decides on Wednesday after India's close, so Thursday, alongside the first ₹50,000 crore bond sale, is the first session able to trade it.
