The names worth knowing from Monday.

- Ratnamani Metals & Tubes had the day's biggest move, up 14.55% to ₹2,697, after a subsidiary won US$286 million of export orders, though the filing names the buyer only as International Customers and does not say how much of the work is sub-contracted and sold on a trading margin rather than made in-house.

- Vishal Mega Mart rose 9.74% to ₹113.50 after its board re-appointed Gunender Kapur as Managing Director and Chief Executive for five years from 1 September, settling who runs the recently listed retailer ten months early, and the filing went out on Friday evening so Monday was the first session that could price it.

- IDBI Bank rose 6.92% with nothing filed, a ₹94,750 crore bank pulling away from its own sector by about eight percentage points on the day state-owned banks led the market lower, and Aegis Logistics fell 5.39% with nothing established either, so five of the twelve biggest movers had no cause anyone could point to.
