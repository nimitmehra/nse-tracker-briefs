A few cross-currents from Monday worth thinking about.

- Indian shares were reported down because Iran threatened to fine, seize or confiscate ships crossing the Strait of Hormuz, but Brent fell 1.81% and Indian refiners rose on cheaper crude, with Indian Oil up 1.91%, which is not how an oil-importing market behaves when it believes a supply shock is coming.

- The American sanctions announcement behind much of that coverage landed between roughly 17:30 and 19:00 India time, after Indian shares had shut, so Monday traded the anticipation and not the contents.

- Gold rose 1.70% and split the gold trade rather than lifting all of it, since gold lenders and smaller jewellers ran with the metal while Titan and Kalyan Jewellers sat out, because a higher price is good for a lender holding pledged gold and two-sided for a jeweller, who books an inventory gain now and loses volume later if the price stays there.
