A few cross-currents from Monday worth thinking about.

Cheaper crude is not uniformly good for everything called energy. A producer sells the barrel rather than buying it, so Oil and Natural Gas Corporation fell 0.22% into a broad rally. The cleanest beneficiary was not an energy company at all: InterGlobe Aviation rose 4.43%, because fuel is roughly a third of an Indian airline's cost base.

The record car month was bought one layer down the chain. July passenger-vehicle wholesales set an all-time record, up 33%, but the makers announce those volumes themselves, so the median parts supplier rose 3.83% against 2.26% for the makers, and Maruti Suzuki closed 0.59% lower on its own record month. These are dispatches to dealers anyway, not sales to customers.

India VIX, the market's fear gauge, rose 1.40% to 11.93 on a rising day with a rate decision two days away, and gold did not fall on the peace headline that knocked crude down.
