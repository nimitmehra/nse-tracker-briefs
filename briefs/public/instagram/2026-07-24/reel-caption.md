A fifth straight down day. The Nifty slipped 0.43% to 23,767.45, defensive into the weekend as the 10% US tariff surcharge lapsed on Friday but brought no relief, and near-$100 oil kept the pressure on. Domestic funds bought ₹5,454 crore against ₹3,893 crore of foreign selling, which is the only reason the fall stayed shallow.

Under the surface, the day rewarded the future, not the past. PVR INOX rose 5.61% on a real turnaround, swinging to a ₹56 crore profit and turning net-cash positive. Spandana fell 10.52%, the day's biggest drop, on a thin, unconvincing quarter. Even Acutaas (revenue up 59%) fell 5.8% because guidance held flat on a 69x valuation.

What to watch: 1 August, the US reciprocal-tariff deadline, deal still unsigned.
