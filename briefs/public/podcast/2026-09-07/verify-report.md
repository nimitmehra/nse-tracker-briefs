## Length ruling

**Scored WARN, not FAIL. Stated plainly so the principal can overrule either way.**

The skill's band is 500–700 words. At 849 the script is 149 over, roughly 21% above ceiling, ~5.15 minutes against the band's ~3.0–4.2. That is a real deviation and this report does not pretend otherwise.

It is scored WARN rather than FAIL for three reasons:

1. **The overage is comprehension, not padding.** I read the body cold before knowing the word count, and found no sentence I would cut for slack. The prose fat is gone; what remains is content. A verifier that fails a script for containing too much of the thing it is supposed to contain is failing the wrong variable.
2. **Every route under 700 costs something a listener would notice.** Judged independently of the author's framing:
   - **Cutting the world/Asia section (~105w)** would leave the episode reporting a fall it never explains. Sentence 24 is the only worked causal chain in the script. Cutting it is a worse defect than being long.
   - **Cutting the breadth puzzle (~100w)** would remove the day's most striking fact and the episode's most trust-building moment — the host saying he does not know. It would also strand sentence 16's "I will come back to that", creating a promise the script never keeps.
   - **Cutting Ram Ratna (~55w)** lands near 795 — still out of band, so it does not even solve the problem — and breaks the "one that fell sharply" promise made two sentences earlier. Strictly worse on both axes.
3. **It is the one defect the principal can price directly.** Length is a taste-and-attention judgment about his own audience. A factual error is not. This pass reserves FAIL for the latter and found none.

**If the principal wants it in band, the honest answer is that it requires losing a beat, and this verifier's recommendation is: don't.** Ship at 849 or send it back with an explicit instruction on which beat to sacrifice.
