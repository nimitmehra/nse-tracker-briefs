## Episode title

**7 Sep 2026 | NSE Daily Brief: The market fell and nobody was selling**

## Description

Oil and interest rates both moved over a weekend India was shut, and only one of them moved Indian share prices. Brent crude is reported near 97 dollars after the heaviest tanker strikes yet in the US–Iran war, about 9% above 28 August. But Monday's fall came from Friday's American jobs number, which repriced the odds of a US rate rise. The Nifty 50 closed at 23,779.15, down 0.50%, with Indian software shares down 2.28%. America was shut for Labor Day, so any comparison you read this week between Monday's Nifty and "US markets" is comparing Monday's India against Friday's America. And the counter-intuitive part: 453 of 710 shares fell while neither foreign nor domestic institutions were selling. We do not know why, and we say so.

## Key points

- **Brent reported near US$97**, roughly 9% above 28 August, after the largest exchange of strikes on tankers yet in the US–Iran war. Web-reported, not confirmed from our own data.
- **Nifty 50 −0.50% to 23,779.15; Nifty IT −2.28%**, a fourth straight down session. The payroll-to-software link is the read every carrier ran, not a confirmed cause.
- **America was shut on Monday** for Labor Day. Every India-versus-US comparison this week is Monday against Friday.
- **Asia went the other way, hard** — Korea's KOSPI +4.61%, Japan's Nikkei about +2.07% — because North Asia sells AI-buildout equipment while Indian software sells project work to US clients.
- **453 of 710 shares fell and neither institutional bloc sold** — FIIs bought a net ₹280.1 cr, DIIs ₹566.8 cr. Unexplained, and left unexplained on purpose.
- **TBZ +9.99%** on GRT Jewellers' agreement to acquire 74.12%; the open offer to other shareholders is ₹249.61 while the stock closed at ₹528.90.
- **Three non-moves:** IndiGo +0.48% on a war-premium oil day, gold lenders lower as gold rose 1.06%, and the rupee unchanged at ₹94.485 through both shocks.
- **US rate decision 15–16 September.** Odds of a rise run 52–65% across venues. We publish the range, not a point estimate.
