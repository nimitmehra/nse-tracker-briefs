## Episode title

**3 August 2026 | NSE Daily Brief: Two indices, one rally, ninety basis points apart**

## Description (Spotify episode page)

India's closing prices are calculated a new way from Monday morning, and by the bell the Sensex and the Nifty disagreed about the size of the day's rally by 90 basis points — the second-widest gap since 2010. Neither number is wrong. The honest answer is breadth: 534 of 680 stocks rose, 144 fell, and the median stock gained 1.35%. What lifted them was crude, down 7.30% to $83.54 on an OPEC+ supply increase and a US-Iran de-escalation story Iran itself denies. Plus Zee, Muthoot Finance, and the seven moves nobody can explain.

## Key points

- **The mechanism changed on day one.** Under a SEBI circular effective 03-August, the closing price of every NSE- and BSE-listed stock with futures and options contracts now comes from a single call auction between 15:15 and 15:30, replacing the old average of the last 30 minutes. Orders above the prevailing price in a few heavyweight Nifty names are enough to lift an index close. The Sensex closed +0.70%, the Nifty +1.60%, and both are valid outputs of the same session. Phase 2, a pre-open auction, is scheduled for 07-September.
- **Breadth is the honest measure of the day.** Nifty 50 at 24,774.30; 534 of 680 stocks up, 144 down, median +1.35%; Nifty Midcap and Smallcap each about +1.2%; 74 names at 52-week closing highs. Nifty IT rose 3.28%, the strongest reading on the board, with no catalyst that could be dated to Monday.
- **Crude did the lifting, and half the reason is contested.** Brent fell 7.30% over the weekend, from $90.12 on Friday to $83.54, on an OPEC+ September output increase and a called-off US strike on Iran. Confirmed: the strike was called off. Not confirmed: any deal. Iran's foreign ministry said on 03-August that there are no negotiations with the United States, and the Strait of Hormuz remains physically restricted, with passage requiring Iranian permission. India imports roughly 85% of the crude it burns, so the price fall is real relief regardless of which half of the story survives.
- **Fear rose on an up day.** India VIX gained 1.40% to 11.93 on a strongly rising market, sitting 3.25% above its 52-week low two days before a rate decision.
- **The movers.** Zee Entertainment −14.33%, the biggest move on the board in either direction, after SEBI's final order dated 31-July barring the company from the securities market for two months and its two promoters for a year each, over the unauthorised 2016 pledge of company land to secure group borrowings — on the same day shareholders approved a preferential warrant issue to those promoters at ₹126, against Monday's ₹98.14 close. Muthoot Finance −7.33% on a quarter where profit rose 43% but the lending margin fell 174 bps year-on-year and profit was 16.9% below the previous quarter. Urban Company +12.98% on revenue up 44% and order volumes up 79%, still loss-making at ₹92 crore.
- **Seven moves have no established cause.** Five of the ten biggest gainers (Indo Borax, Balrampur Chini, Pine Labs, Deep Industries, Aegis Logistics) and the day's most watchable fall, JNK India at −6.10% against its own capital-goods group at +2.19%, were checked against exchange filings, dated broker actions and index events, and no cause was established. CAMS, Persistent Systems and Dhanuka Agritech reacted in ways no source explains. The brief states this rather than fitting a story to the headline.
- **Next:** the RBI's rate decision on Wednesday 05-August, with the repo held at 5.25% across three meetings and economist polls unanimous for no change, so the language and the inflation forecast carry more information than the rate. Wednesday also brings the July services survey. Tuesday's second auction close tells you whether the index gap was teething or structural.
