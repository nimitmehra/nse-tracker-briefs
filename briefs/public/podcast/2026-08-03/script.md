Good evening. This is India Markets Brief from toroIQ. Your read on today's session.

The market went up today, and the two numbers everybody quotes disagreed about by how much. The Sensex rose zero point seven zero percent. The Nifty rose one point six zero percent. And neither is wrong.

Here is why. The way India's closing prices are worked out changed this morning. Until Friday, a stock's close was an average of the last half hour of trading. From today, for stocks with futures contracts, it comes out of a single auction between quarter past three and half past three. On day one, brokers say, some participants bid above the going price in a few heavyweight Nifty names, and that alone can lift an index close. So that gap, nine tenths of one percent, is day-one plumbing, not a signal. It is the second widest gap since twenty ten.
