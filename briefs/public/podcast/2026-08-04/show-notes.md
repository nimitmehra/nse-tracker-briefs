**Episode title:** 04 August 2026 | NSE Daily Brief: the RBI decides tomorrow, and its inflation forecast still assumes $95 oil

**Description**

The Reserve Bank of India announces its rate decision at 10:00 tomorrow, and the number worth watching is not the rate. In June the Bank raised its inflation forecast for the year to 5.1%, built on Brent crude averaging about $95 a barrel. Brent has traded between $79 and $86 all week. But the weather office now forecasts below-normal August rain at 94% of the long-period average, so one input has moved the other way. Indian shares fell into the decision, the Nifty 50 by 0.64%, with the fear gauge up 2.18%.

**In this episode**

- Why the market fell and fear rose at the same time: 279 shares up against 392 down, out of 680 tracked.
- Day three of the new closing auction, and the number that explains it: auction turnover of ₹1,542 cr on the NSE against ₹9 cr on the BSE, roughly 165 to one. Two indices can disagree on plumbing alone. It is not a market signal.
- Crude's round trip: near $86 and up ~2.9% during Indian market hours, $79 by 21:15 IST. Only the first belongs to today's session, and every "rising oil" line in tonight's close reports was written off it.
- Greaves Cotton fell 14.04% on revenue up 31% and net profit down 22%. A widely-ranking article headlined a 220% profit jump is dated July 2025 and describes last year's quarter.
- Ather Energy rose 13.96% after its first-ever positive operating profit. It is still a loss-making company.
- Property was the day's worst corner on the eve of a rate decision, and the cause was earnings rather than rates: three of the five worst names had filed weak results, and DLF's new sales bookings fell about 94%.
- The honest gap: CG Power rose 6.53% with no filing on either 03 or 04 August and nothing dated to today. Nine of the 32 moves graded in tonight's brief have no cause established at primary source.
