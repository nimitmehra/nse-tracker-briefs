## Episode title

**2026-08-06 | NSE Daily Brief: The market paid for margins and ignored growth**

## Description

Five Indian companies filed strong quarterly results on Thursday and the market bought none of them. RateGain grew revenue 188% and fell. Garware Hi-Tech Films posted its best quarter ever and fell. Samvardhana Motherson lifted profit 77.5% and went nowhere. Meanwhile the two heavy falls, Firstsource Solutions −13.40% and Blue Star −5.65%, were both margin collapse inside growing revenue. The Nifty closed up 0.05% but 371 stocks fell against 302 that rose, and the index finished green on two names. We could not establish a cause for the largest of them, and we say so.

## Key points

- **Nifty 50 closed at 24,636.00 (+0.05%)**, Sensex +0.48%, Bank Nifty +0.56%, Nifty IT −0.95% and the day's worst measurable sector. Underneath, 302 stocks rose and 371 fell, the median stock was −0.22%, and the Nifty 50 was the weakest cohort with 30 of its members down.
- **Reliance Industries rose 3.52%** and, at roughly 8% of the index, is worth more than the whole day's gain on its own. **No cause dated to today could be established.** The close is corroborated; the reason is not, and we do not supply one.
- **The earnings verdict.** Strong numbers were not paid for: RATEGAIN −3.42%, SJS −2.89%, GRWRHITECH −0.96%, EMCURE −1.14%, MOTHERSON +0.42%. Margin damage was punished hard: FSL −13.40% (revenue +22.9%, profit −2.0% on an exceptional charge, operating cash flow −72%) and BLUESTARCO −5.65% (revenue +13.3%, profit −15.2%, cooling-segment margin halved to 2.9%). Navin Fluorine, with a 34.2% margin, was the exception and closed at a lifetime high.
- **Macro.** The Reserve Bank held the repo rate at 5.25% on Wednesday, cut its inflation forecast to 5.0% and raised growth to 6.7%. The July services PMI landed the same morning at 53.3, a 53-month low, in the part of the economy producing about 55% of India's output.
- **The wedge.** The rate signal reached lenders and never reached property. Shriram Finance +11.08%, Cholamandalam +9.37% and Bajaj Finance +8.67% on the week; Lodha −5.68% on the week with DLF, Oberoi Realty, Prestige and Godrej Properties all lower. Same signal, opposite outcomes, reason not established. Foreign investors sold on both sessions, and stripping out the single 04-August session leaves August-to-date foreign flow at −₹40 crore.
