## Episode title

**30 July 2026 | NSE Daily Brief: the market priced where profits came from, not how big they were**

## Description

India's headline indices rose on Thursday while two out of every three stocks fell. The Nifty 50 closed 0.28% higher at 24,317.15 and the Sensex 0.35% higher, but 457 shares declined against 215 that advanced, the median stock fell 0.60%, and India VIX rose on an up day. The market bought the earnings and cheaper-crude story and sold anything tied to the rate path, four sessions before the Reserve Bank decides. And in three separate results, what moved the share was not the size of the profit but where it came from.

## Key points

- **Index up, market down.** Nifty +0.28% to 24,317.15, Sensex +0.35%, but breadth was 457 declines to 215 advances and the median stock fell 0.60%. India VIX rose 1.21% on a rising market.
- **Rates were the thing being sold.** The RBI meets 3-5 August with the repo at 5.25%; a Reuters poll of 72 economists reads 68 expecting a hold, four a hike and none a cut. Property developers were the weakest group, with insurers and non-bank lenders behind them; banks barely moved.
- **Auto carried the index** on Mahindra & Mahindra's quarter, profit up about 34% with return on equity at 23% while absorbing 400-500 bps of raw-material inflation. Thursday was also the BSE Sensex monthly expiry, not an NSE one, which concentrates trading in the largest index names.
- **The quality-of-the-number trio.** PCBL Chemical fell 10.31% on profit up 65%, roughly ₹70 cr of which was a one-off inventory gain. Hexaware fell 6.91% though its operating margin expanded 352 bps, with the entire profit decline sitting below the operating line. KPIT Technologies fell 7.67% on guidance, not on the quarter, after European carmakers cut spending.
- **Movers.** Balkrishna Industries +10.82% on revenue up 25% and profit up 56%. Redington +8.18% to a 52-week high on record revenue, though the company itself credits part of it to higher PC prices caused by a global memory shortage.
- **Friday is the real reading for four results.** Vedanta, Bajaj Finance, Hyundai and Mankind all filed in the last 8 to 21 minutes of the session, so their numbers had no time to trade. Brent eased 1.19% to $89.66 but is still up 22.96% on the month.
