**Description**

A fifth straight down day for Indian shares, the worst losing streak since January. The ten percent US tariff surcharge on Indian exports reached its legal expiry and lapsed on Friday, but brought no relief: Washington is reported to be moving the same duty onto an open-ended footing, the 1 August reciprocal-tariff deadline stays live, and the India-US trade deal is still unsigned. Layered on crude that sat near one hundred dollars all week, the Nifty fell 0.43 percent to 23,767, cushioned only by heavy domestic buying. Inside a busy Q1 results day, the lesson was clear: a good past quarter is not enough if the future looks softer. PVR INOX, Spandana Sphoorty and Syrma SGS led the movers.
