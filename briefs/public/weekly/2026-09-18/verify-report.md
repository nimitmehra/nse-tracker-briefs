## Part 3: punch list

Items 1 to 3 are the FAIL. Items 4 to 13 are WARN-class and should be applied in the same revision.

1. Section 8, Friday. Quoted: "Friday: the Reserve Bank's weekly reserves figure. A second fall of about five billion dollars, with the currency part identified, would be the first time the swap story could no longer hide genuine rupee defence." Why: the first fall is never mentioned in the script, Section 6 says reserves jumped, and "currency part" and "rupee defence" are not glossed; a listener cannot repeat it. Replacement: "Friday: the Reserve Bank's weekly reserves figure. Reserves fell about five billion dollars in the week to 11 September, and the Bank did not say how much of that was dollars sold to support the rupee. A second fall of that size, with the dollar sales spelt out, would be the first sign that the swap money can no longer hide the Reserve Bank spending dollars to defend the rupee. A rise would tell us nothing either way."

2. Section 4, Friday. Quoted: "TCS fell nearly four per cent on the Tata Sons dispute, and TCS is heavier in the thirty-share Sensex than in the fifty-share Nifty." Why: TCS is not placed at first mention and the dispute is not explained until Section 6. Replacement: "TCS, Tata Consultancy Services, the group's software-services company, fell nearly four per cent after the owners of Tata Sons fell out in public on Thursday night, a story I will come to. TCS is heavier in the thirty-share Sensex than in the fifty-share Nifty."

3. Section 4, Tuesday. Quoted: "Metal fell too, but on copper prices, not Indian rates." Why: the cause is given without its direction; the brief says "on a fall in copper prices". Replacement: "Metal fell too, but on a fall in copper prices, not Indian rates."
