## Block 1: the hook

**The mechanism behind the claim.** The three costs are three prices, each of which moved while the Indian exchange was closed for Ganesh Chaturthi on Monday 14 September, so the first session that could react to any of them was Tuesday. Oil: a drone strike on Thursday 10 September shut Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline, the route that carries its crude to the Red Sea and around the closed Strait of Hormuz, and Brent settled at $108.75 on Tuesday, more than 20% higher in September. American rates: the ten-year US Treasury yield touched 5% in Monday's American session for the first time since 2007. Indian rates: the Reserve Bank had announced on Friday 11 September that it would sell ₹1 trillion of government bonds to drain surplus cash, and that pushed India's own ten-year yield to a four-month high of about 7.1%. The Nifty fell 1.19% on Tuesday to 23,118.60, its lowest close in five months.

**Why "three feared events" and "nothing broke".** The Fed raised by 25 basis points to 3.75 to 4.00%, twelve votes to none, its first rise since 2023; the market had priced it at 92 to 94% likely for a week, so it was not a surprise when it arrived. The Bank of Japan raised to 1.25%, the highest since 1995, and the yen fell anyway, which matters because a sharply stronger yen would have forced investors who borrow cheaply in yen to unwind positions, and that has hit Indian shares before. The Reserve Bank's first ₹50,000 crore sale drew ₹66,590 crore of bids and left the ten-year yield at about 7.06%, essentially unchanged. Thursday's close was "little changed" on every wire, and India VIX fell from 13.43 on Tuesday to 11.39 by Friday.

**Likely question: "if everything the market feared happened, why is the index still down on the week?"** Because Tuesday's fall was 1.19% and the three sessions after it added back roughly 0.97% between them; the recovery was nearly complete, not complete. And under the index, more shares fell than rose over the week, 393 against 319, because Tuesday was so one-sided that three ordinary sessions could not reverse it for the typical share.
