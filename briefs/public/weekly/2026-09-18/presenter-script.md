## 1. Hook

Three costs turned against India over a long weekend, and the market paid for all three in one afternoon.

Oil went up, because a drone strike shut Saudi Arabia's main export pipeline. The interest rate on ten-year American government bonds touched five per cent for the first time since 2007. And our own central bank said it would sell a trillion rupees of bonds to pull cash out of the banking system. Tuesday fell more than one per cent to a five-month low.

Then the three things the market feared most all happened. An American rate rise. A Japanese rate rise. The first Indian bond sale. And nothing broke. By Friday the market had clawed back nearly all of Tuesday.

That is the week. Let me take you through why.
