## What Happened

### A subsidiary leaves the company at face value

Waaree Renewable Technologies (WAAREERTL) told the exchange at 18:29 on Thursday evening that its board approved transferring its entire shareholding in a wholly-owned subsidiary to **Waaree Energies — the holding company that is also its promoter.** The price is **₹1,00,000**, and the filing says the shares will be sold at face value of ₹10 each. That subsidiary had just been renamed from **Sunsantional Energy Private Limited to Waaree Green Data Centers Private Limited.** The pricing basis the company offers is last year's contribution to turnover and net worth — "Nil / Negligible" — and the word valuation appears nowhere in the document. The filing is compliant on its face and declares the connected-party status openly. **The flag is the absent valuation and the backward-looking price, not an allegation of wrongdoing.** A rename into the data-centre theme is not a backward-looking fact, and the document was public through a full session while the stock closed +0.43%.

### We used the wrong clock, and today proved it

Yesterday this brief led on India having sat out the biggest company result in the world, while Korea rose 1.53% and Nifty IT fell 0.32%. **The reporting was right and the framing was wrong, so here is the correction.** Nvidia reported on Wednesday after the US close; on Thursday India traded only the press release, because the US market had not yet opened to vote on it. **That vote came in the Thursday US cash session, which closed at 01:30 IST this morning — Nasdaq +1.57%, Nvidia +8.7%, technology the only S&P 500 sector to advance.** India opened on Friday against that close and Nifty IT gained 3.51%. There was never a lag; we measured against a press release when the clock Indian IT tracks is the US close.

### The IT rally is beta, and the hedge is bypassed

Yesterday we published that chip demand converting into Indian services revenue "is our reasoning and not a sourced finding." **Today does not lift that hedge — it makes it unnecessary.** Thursday's US session was a software rally as much as a chip one, with Salesforce up 22% and Okta up 28%, and Indian services sell into enterprise software budgets rather than into silicon. **Four things say beta rather than fundamentals.** Participation was uniform, including Wipro +2.58% and Mphasis +1.72%, names with no AI exposure. The leaders were mid-cap beta — Coforge +5.93%, Persistent +3.91%. The rupee moved the wrong way at −0.07%, a headwind for exporters. And **not one sell-side desk published a sector note all day**, which is not what a desk does when earnings power has changed.
