## What Happened

### India's biggest bank has eight weeks to find a chief

**Sashidhar Jagdishan told HDFC Bank's board over the weekend that he will not seek reappointment, and he retires as managing director and chief executive on 26-October-2026.** The mechanics are the story. Deputy managing director **Kaizad Bharucha**, three decades at the bank and the obvious internal candidate, has already served twelve years on the board — and the Reserve Bank caps a chief executive's continuous tenure at fifteen. Appoint him and he cannot finish a three-year term. The nominations committee has been asked to find a successor within two months, against a term that ends in less than two, and the bank is reported to be hiring a search firm and weighing outside candidates. **No successor is decided, and no name should be treated as one.**
