## What Happened

### The statement carries the variance; the rate carries none

The Reserve Bank of India's six-member rate-setting committee decides at 10:00 tomorrow morning, and the repo rate has stood at 5.25% since June. A fourth consecutive hold is what every poll located today points to, and **no respondent anywhere expects a cut** — so the decision itself has almost no variance left in it. The variance sits in a single number. The committee raised its own FY27 retail-inflation estimate to **5.1% from 4.6%** on energy pass-through, and that energy input has since fallen roughly **9.5% in two sessions**. A hold with a downward revision to that projection is dovish substance inside a neutral decision, and it is the one outcome nothing in positioning is prepared for. Read tomorrow as a statement, not a rate.

### Jio Financial's ₹7,200 crore vote is now formally on the clock

Jio Financial Services (JIOFIN), a holding, published the newspaper version of its annual-meeting notice at 11:43 today. It carries no new substance, but it is the statutory publication, and it starts the clock: **record date 10 August, electronic voting closes 19 August, meeting 26 August.** On that agenda sits authorisation for **₹7,200 crore of related-party dealings with Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund — 204.94% of last year's consolidated revenue** — split across three tables whose ratios are never added up, with **₹750 crore already deployed ahead of the vote.** The graph moved that watch to elevated tonight, the first elevated trigger anywhere in the book. One correction must travel with it: **the fourth resolution appoints PKF Sridhar & Santhanam as a joint auditor alongside Deloitte Haskins & Sells. That is an addition. Deloitte continues, and no auditor is walking away.**

### The index fell on its own plumbing, not on news

Three explanations were offered for a 0.64% fall and two of them are mechanical. The weekly derivatives expiry ran today, a Tuesday. The Closing Auction Session — the 20-minute call auction that has set closing prices for derivative-eligible stocks since Monday — was only on its second day. Only the third explanation, caution before tomorrow's decision, is an information event, and it has not happened yet. The evidence for the mechanical read sits in the index gap: **Monday's 90 basis points between Nifty and Sensex narrowed to 37 today and flipped sign**, which is Monday's inflated Nifty close unwinding. That makes the **Sensex's −0.27% the cleaner read** on the day's actual deterioration. It is microstructure, it is not a signal, and nothing below rests on it.

### The strait got worse while the oil price fell

Brent fell a further **2.65% to $81.55**, about 9.5% across two sessions, and the situation underneath it deteriorated rather than improved. **A cargo ship was struck 37 km north-east of Al Khasab at 02:00 this morning — the third vessel hit in four days.** Iran's foreign ministry states flatly that it has no negotiations with the United States and that the talks are with Oman, over a transit route, and that the open questions are who controls the strait and who collects the transit fees. **That is a fee-and-sovereignty negotiation, not a de-escalation**, and for a third consecutive run nobody published any scepticism of the de-escalation framing. The read-through for the principal is narrow and it is tomorrow's: cheaper crude is precisely what makes a 5.1% inflation projection hard to defend.
