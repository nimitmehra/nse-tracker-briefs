## What Happened

### The market bought "priced-in"; the bill arrives tomorrow

Nifty rose 0.43% to 23,217.60 in a 23,116–23,285 range, but the advance was narrow: BSE advances to declines ran 0.84 and the Smallcap 250 bought back a −2.06% morning to finish −0.16%. Life insurers, State Bank and FMCG led — the NSE selling-shareholder complex, on the day the exchange's own anchor book was cut to ₹6,250 crore because, the issuer said, demand was too large. IT gave back Tuesday's essay bid in full. Every close wrap said the same thing, correctly: the Federal Reserve decides at 23:30 IST, after the close, so nothing today can be attributed to it. Thursday is the crowded day — first post-Fed session, the RBI's first ₹50,000 crore bond sale, NSE bidding, weekly expiry. Today was the coil, not the spring.

### The RBI drains ₹1 trillion into the Fed's week

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) absorbed ₹2.90 trillion today at variable-rate reverse repo — banks parking surplus cash with the central bank — at a 5.24% cut-off against a 5.25% repo; its second ₹1 trillion auction drew only ₹40,302 crore. Banks would rather hold the cash, so the surplus, about ₹9.85 trillion, is sticky. What removes it durably is the ₹1 trillion open-market sale of government bonds: ₹50,000 crore tomorrow 17-September, then ₹25,000 crore each on 21 and 28 September, the first large net sale in two years. Wholesale prices came in at 9.92% for August on holiday Monday (fuel and power 22.93%), a 500-basis-point wedge over retail inflation at 4.82%. The ten-year eased 4 bps to about 7.05% today; that is pre-supply relief, not the supply passing.

### UPI's first fee lands on Jio Financial's payments bank

The National Payments Corporation of India's steering committee decided on Tuesday to levy a 0.4% merchant discount rate (MDR) — the fee a merchant pays its bank — on person-to-merchant UPI payments above ₹2,000 from 15-October. The fee is capped at ₹300, with a flat ₹5 for telecom, rail, fuel and insurance. Payments up to ₹2,000 and person-to-person transfers stay free under a Monday government notification. Transactions above ₹2,000 were 4% of UPI volume but two-thirds of value last year. Jio Financial Services (JIOFIN) owns Jio Payments Bank outright and runs UPI on its app, so it gains a revenue line where none existed. The size is unknowable until the fee split is published, and the stock closed ₹224.84, 0.57% above its 52-week low — the market saying "not enough, not soon".

### Both Gulf oil routes constrained; no Indian desk carried it

The Iran–Gulf meeting in Salalah on reopening the Strait of Hormuz — the announcement behind Brent's −2.8% relief on 11-September — was postponed indefinitely by Oman's foreign minister, and Washington wants any talks refocused on Tehran's nuclear programme. Saudi Arabia's East–West pipeline, the Hormuz bypass, is still shut; the restart estimate runs from "days" (the US Energy Secretary) to "five to six weeks" (CNBC's sources), and the range is carried, not resolved. Brent settled $108.75 on Tuesday, up more than 20% in September, and traded $107.56 at the Indian close — softer intraday, which is all "oil eased" meant. For an importer whose April–August crude bill is $17.5 billion higher than last year, the off-ramp priced on 11-September is gone; the rupee at 95.945 is where that shows.

### KPI Green's reaffirmed rating names ₹2,750 crore of new debt

KPI Green Energy (KPIGREEN) filed at 18:15, after the close, a CRISIL reaffirmation of AA+ (CE)/Stable on its ₹670 crore guaranteed bonds (the attachment says "ICRA"; the body is CRISIL). From the primary: the enhanced rating is unchanged; the issuer's standalone rating is CRISIL A, four notches lower; and the company "plans to undertake large debt funded capex of around Rs 2750 crore" for wind and battery projects over one to two years. Interest cover fell to 5.5 times from 7.1. The rationale is silent on the ₹6,500 crore related-party ballot closing 24-September, though that subsidiary sits inside its perimeter — a reaffirmation is not a governance clearance. The stock closed ₹276.35, its 52-week low for a third run; a post-close filing cannot explain a 15:30 price.
