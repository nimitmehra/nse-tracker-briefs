## What Happened

### The oil-and-rates regime hardened, and today India finally priced it

Nifty fell 1.19% to 23,118.60 and closed at the day's low, after an IT-led gap-up. The shock built while Mumbai was shut: drones from Iraq hit Saudi Arabia's East–West pipeline, the Hormuz bypass, and Riyadh closed it as a precaution. Brent settled at $105.68 on Monday and traded near $107.4 through the Indian session, so "Nifty fell as oil rose" is valid on direction and timing today. The US ten-year printed 5.04%, its highest since 2007, and futures put Wednesday's Fed hike near 94%. The amplifier was domestic: the RBI's ₹1 trillion bond-sale programme starts Thursday, the Indian ten-year touched a four-month high, and rate-sensitive realty fell 4%. Mid and small caps fell 2.1% and 2.4% against the index's 1.2% — that gap is where your book lives.

### The last-hour reversal has no author, and the calendar is now crowded

The Sensex was up about 587 points intraday and finished down 778, a swing of roughly 1,365 points in the final leg. No Indian outlet located a catalyst, and both the 5% ten-year and the $107 Brent were known at 09:15. The honest reading is positioning ahead of Wednesday, not news; what matters for you is the sequencing. The Fed decides at about 23:30 IST Wednesday, after tomorrow's close, so Thursday is the first session that can trade it, and Thursday also carries the first ₹50,000 crore bond-sale tranche. Further out, the GST Council did not meet on 12-September; it was pushed to 7-October for the BRICS summit, onto the RBI's own decision day. Infosys (INFY) rose 3.79% to ₹1,077.00, the only holding that went up, and Nifty IT was the only green sector at +2.19%. That is rhetoric from three executives and a presidential rebuttal, nothing enacted. The sector opened up 5.2% and gave back more than half by the close. A weaker rupee at 95.94 is a genuine translation tailwind and the sounder leg. The read-through: one session of relative strength on a 24-hour story, on the day the rate leg of the bear case got worse. The thesis is unchanged, and Infosys still carries zero triggers.
