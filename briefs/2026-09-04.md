## What Happened

### The cables repricing took its second and larger leg

**UltraTech Cement filed its "Ultravolt" wires-and-cables launch with the exchange at 18:29:53 on 3-September — after that day's close and before this one's open — which makes it a valid cause for today's move rather than a plausible one.** The commitment is ₹1,800 crore, of which ₹888 crore is already spent, for 10.98 lakh kilometres of capacity at Bharuch, in commercial production since 1-September. On day one that makes the Aditya Birla group the second-largest wires producer in the country. **Today the incumbents were sold: KEI −8.95% to ₹4,850, RR Kabel −6.25% to ₹2,454.10, Polycab −5.76% to ₹8,300, Havells −3.83%, Finolex Cables −3.46% to ₹1,220.20 — and the entrant, UltraTech itself, rose 1.18%.** A new competitor is not a demand miss, and the market repriced it as neither.

### The market cut what it will pay, not what it expects them to earn

**This is a supply shock, and the mechanism is the multiple rather than the estimate.** Nobody cut a revenue forecast today; what changed is what a rupee of cable profit is worth once the second-largest producer is a cement group with a balance sheet.
