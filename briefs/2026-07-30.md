## What Happened

### The index rose and two of three stocks fell
Nifty closed up 0.28% at 24,317.15, and almost nothing else in the session agreed with it. Across 676 priced instruments there were **215 advances against 457 declines**, an advance-decline ratio of **0.47**; the **median stock fell 0.60%**; the MidCap index fell 0.35% and the SmallCap 0.56%. Yesterday the same three readings were +1.10%, +0.82% and +1.48% — broad and genuine. One session later the breadth has inverted, and **India VIX rose 1.21% on an up day**, which is the market paying up for protection while the headline holds. Two of the desk's own agents found this independently and no outlet in either scan reported it. It is one session, not a regime, but it lands four days before the 3–5 August rate meeting.

### Five stale BUYs were refreshed and all five failed
The research desk refreshed its five oldest high-conviction BUYs today and downgraded every one. **APARINDS** went BUY 7.5 to PASS 3 — 76% of the increase in operating profit was a per-unit price gain while volumes fell 13.7%, so 78% reported growth normalises to about 10%. **HINDZINC** went to PASS 3 on the company's own slide 30, which shows **90.3% of the profit increase was price and currency** against 6.8% from volume. **PNGJL** to HOLD 5 — same-store sales up 46.1% against gold up 63%, implying tonnage down 14%. **LODHA** to HOLD 5 — 44% of profit was land sales. Those four failed on one mechanism: growth accepted at the revenue line without separating price from volume. The fifth, **PERSISTENT**, passed the volume test and failed on something else entirely. That is a finding about the prior research, not about five companies.

### The tariff binary closed on 24 July, and worse than it reads
Yesterday's brief carried an unsigned 1-August tariff deadline as the live binary on GRWRHITECH. **It is not a binary and it closed six days ago.** The US trade representative's final Section 301 determination, published 23 July, took effect 24 July and assigns Indian goods **10%** — cut from the 12.5% bracket after India amended its foreign trade policy. The 10% emergency rate it replaced lapsed the same day at its statutory 150-day cap. So the headline rate is unchanged across the handover, but the legal basis moved from a time-capped measure counting down to a finding **with no expiry**. For an exporter that is worse than "unchanged" implies, and it is the opposite of relief. The underlying trade agreement is still unsigned. The determination's own text could not be machine-read; this rests on four trade-law advisories.

### KPI Green was not silent — it commissioned 200 MW
Yesterday's brief described KPI Green Energy (KPIGREEN), a holding, as quiet against a loud renewables cohort. **That is wrong and is retracted.** On 20 July it commissioned **200 MW(AC) / 269 MW(DC)** of solar for Coal India at Khavda, state-agency certified and filed to the exchanges, with a further 100 MW(AC) still under execution — confirmed across five sources. The accurate statement is narrower and worse: the operations were real and the market paid nothing for them, +0.87% on a +1.10% day, and down on the week and the month. Three days on, the 48.48% institutional vote against re-appointing its independent director has still drawn no press coverage, no company clarification and no exchange query. The node carries one trigger and it is already resolved.

### Waaree's parent grew 79% while the subsidiary shrank
Waaree Energies, the listed parent of holding Waaree Renewable Technologies (WAAREERTL), reported June-quarter revenue up **79.22% year on year** to ₹7,931.79 crore, with profit up 15.39% and operating margin down 438 basis points. In the same quarter WAAREERTL's own revenue fell **16.16% sequentially** to ₹924.25 crore and profit fell 25.58%. That removes end-market demand as an explanation for the subsidiary's contraction and leaves execution phasing, order-book quality, or the terms on which work moves inside the group. The critical review already flagged that its largest June award came from a wholly-owned subsidiary rather than an outside customer. **The intra-group revenue question now outranks the margin binary** the desk has been waiting on — and the margin did not breach anything: 18.77% sits above both the 15% floor management promised and the 16% exit level.

**No derived sector cascades were written, and that remains the correct answer rather than an empty run.** The script was executed in preview only.
