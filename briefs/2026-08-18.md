## What Happened

### One scheme, two holdings, opposite sides of it

**Acutaas Chemicals rose 6.04% to ₹3,391.40 on a filing made at 10:53 in the morning, mid-session** — formal approval under the government's electronics-components manufacturing scheme for an electrolyte-additives plant at Jhagadia in Gujarat. **Read the body, not the headline.** The circulating number, ₹256.47 crore, is the total project outlay; eligible capital spend is ₹119.12 crore, and the incentive is up to a quarter of that through FY2031 — **about ₹29.78 crore, roughly one-eighth of the headline.** Thirty crore spread over five years does not re-rate a company this size by 6% in a session. The approval letter was handed over by **Engineers India, acting as the ministry's project management agency for the whole programme** — 106 sanctioned projects and ₹69,548 crore to date. **Two holdings on opposite sides of one policy instrument, and until tonight the graph had no link between them.** This matters more for Engineers India than the fee will: consultancy is where its operating margin doubled from 7% to 14% last quarter, and administering a national scheme is exactly that kind of low-capital, fee-based work. **We learned it sideways, through the counterparty's filing — the mandate is not mentioned once in Engineers India's own 19-page June-quarter call.** No fee number is disclosed and none is invented here. **That commitment is now recorded as silently dropped.** It was quantified and structural, its check date of 15-August is three days past, and it has now been tested against both transcripts published since it was made. **The word "fund-based" does not appear once in either of them.** And this is not passive silence: **the directly adjacent question was asked in both calls and answered around the commitment on each occasion.** In the March-quarter call an analyst asked directly whether there was "any new form of working capital" behind rising receivables and inventory; the finance chief answered on inventory being project-specific and did not restate the position. No breach is alleged and none is recorded.** The flip is on silence, which is exactly what the rule tests — a previously quantified public commitment, unmentioned across two consecutive calls, past its own check date, with the adjacent question put to management both times. **Forward flag: the next transcript is the one to read for whether the position is restated, withdrawn or simply left where it now is.**
